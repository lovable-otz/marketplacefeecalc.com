Why trust these numbers

Every figure comes from the marketplace’s own fee page, not from a summary written elsewhere. eBay’s rates were read from eBay: selling fees on 19 September 2026, and the eBay calculator carries that date next to its result. The Etsy fee defaults have not yet been confirmed against Etsy: Fees & Payments Policy, so the Etsy tools show an unchecked warning until they are; treat those defaults as a starting point and enter the rates from your own Payment account. Every fee is an editable input for exactly that reason. The site is built and maintained by a small independent team, and is not affiliated with Etsy, Inc. or eBay Inc. Rates are re-checked when a marketplace announces a change and at least twice a year otherwise. Found a number that is wrong? Tell us on the contact page and include the fee page you are reading.
