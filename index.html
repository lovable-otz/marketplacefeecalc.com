Why trust these numbers

Every figure is traced to a named source and carries the date it was checked. eBay’s rates were read from eBay: selling fees on 19 September 2026, and the eBay calculator carries that date next to its result. The Etsy rates were cross-checked on 21 September 2026 against several independent references, which agreed on every figure: $0.20 to list, 6.5% on the total the buyer pays, and 3% + $0.25 processing for US sellers. Etsy: Fees & Payments Policy blocks automated checks, so unlike eBay those figures have not been read from Etsy’s own page; treat them as a starting point and enter the rates from your own Payment account. Every fee is an editable input for exactly that reason. The site is built and maintained by a small independent team, and is not affiliated with Etsy, Inc. or eBay Inc. Rates are re-checked when a marketplace announces a change and at least twice a year otherwise. Found a number that is wrong? Tell us on the contact page and include the fee page you are reading.

What you can work out here

Each tool below answers one question with your own numbers. These are the headline figures behind them.

eBay Fee Calculator
eBay’s final value fee is 13.6% of the total amount of the sale in most categories, on the first $7,500 per item, plus a per-order fee of $0.30 on orders of $10.00 or less and $0.40 on orders above $10.00.
Etsy Fee Calculator
Etsy takes about 10.5% of a typical order, plus fixed cents.
Etsy Fees Explained
Etsy charges three fees on a standard sale: $0.20 per listing, 6.5% of the total the buyer pays including shipping, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing for US sellers.
Etsy Profit Calculator
Profit is what remains after Etsy’s fees and your own costs. On a $100 sale Etsy takes about $9.95, but the number that matters is what is left once materials, postage, packaging and your time come out.
Etsy vs eBay Fees
Etsy charges about 10.5% + $0.45 per order. eBay charges around 13.6% + $0.40 for most categories.

Common questions

How much does eBay take for a fee?

In most categories eBay takes 13.6% of the total amount of the sale, plus a per-order fee of $0.30 on orders of $10.00 or less or $0.40 on larger orders. More in eBay Fee Calculator.

Is the eBay fee 15%?

Not in most categories. The standard final value fee is 13.6%, not 15%. More in eBay Fee Calculator.

How much does eBay take on a $2500 sale?

About $340.40 in a standard 13.6% category, on a $2,500 total with no shipping or tax added. That is 13.6% of $2,500, which is $340.00, plus the $0.40 per-order fee. More in eBay Fee Calculator.

How much are eBay fees on $1200?

About $163.60 in a standard 13.6% category: $163.20 for the final value fee plus the $0.40 per-order fee, on a $1,200 total. In Books, Movies & Music at 15.3% the same sale costs $184.00. More in eBay Fee Calculator.

How much does Etsy take from a $100 sale?

Etsy takes $9.95 from a $100 sale in standard fees, leaving $90.05. That is $6.50 transaction fee (6.5%), $3.25 payment processing (3% + $0.25) and the $0.20 listing fee. More in Etsy Fee Calculator.

Does Etsy charge a $29 fee?

No. Etsy has no standard $29 fee. The figure usually comes from Etsy Plus, an optional subscription that costs $10 a month, or from a run of fees landing on one statement and being read as a single charge. More in Etsy Fee Calculator.
